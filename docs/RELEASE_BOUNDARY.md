# Release boundary

The product emits research hypotheses and validation routes. It does not emit a diagnosis, treatment recommendation, clinical classification, trial eligibility decision, or actionability decision.

Every dossier must carry:

- research-use-only status;
- input and policy versions;
- source and context for every claim;
- support, uncertainty, and negative or missing evidence;
- a replayable event head and content address; and
- a review decision before research release.

The words “specified,” “implemented,” “internally evaluated,” “externally evaluated,” and “research released” have different meanings. A registry entry, fixture, or passing unit test does not establish external scientific validity.
